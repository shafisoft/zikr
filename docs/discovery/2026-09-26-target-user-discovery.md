# Target-User Product Discovery — 2026-09-26

**Method:** A product-discovery pass with a deliberately different brief from the panel
review: start from the target user and the real ritual (not the codebase), research what
users of dhikr/tasbeeh/athkar apps praise and complain about, and generate opportunities
inside the hard constraints ($0/month, offline-first, ≤200KB, privacy brand, en/bn, PWA).
Repo reading was kept light (Idea.md, zikrCatalog.ts, CHANGELOG) by design.

*Caveat: web-search tooling hit its rate cap mid-pass, so some market evidence is from a
smaller set of queries than ideal; the personas rest substantially on the repo's own
catalog choices, which encode real practice patterns.*

---

## User picture

1. **The after-salah tasbih-doer** (the app's deepest-encoded user — the catalog's 33/33/34 + 100x trio exists *only* for this moment). He finishes prayer, picks up fingers or a wooden misbaha, and runs SubhanAllah 33 → Alhamdulillah 33 → Allahu Akbar 34 → tahlil. What tells him it's time is the salah itself; his friction is distraction (losing count mid-set, being interrupted, not remembering whether he did it after Dhuhr). Today he uses fingers — the app's counter already handles counting; nothing in it knows the moment exists.
2. **The morning/evening wird keeper.** After Fajr and before Maghrib she runs a *protective sequence* — the catalog's non-prayer cluster (Bismillahilladhi 3x, Radhitu 3x, Ajirni-minan-nar 7x, Hasbiyallah 7x, Sayyidul Istighfar 1x, Allahumma-a'inni 10x) is literally the Hisn-ul-Muslim morning/evening set. Her unit of success is "did my morning adhkar today," one thing with ten parts and mixed counts — not ten separate goals. She currently juggles a dua book or an adhkar app plus a counter app.
3. **The seasonal pledger.** Ramadan, Dhul-Hijjah's first ten days, or a 40-day istighfar pledge taken after a lecture. Lives in WhatsApp family groups where progress is… screenshot tally charts. Wants a bounded campaign with shared accountability. Zikr's groups (per-zikr shared targets, anonymous contributions) were built for this but nothing curates the *practice* into a starting point.
4. **The Bengali-first user.** Reads bn comfortably; English-first apps feel foreign even when translated. Uses ihadis; gets prayer times from printed নামাজের সময়সূচী calendars. Every catalog entry in `zikrCatalog.ts` carries a bn name and meaning, and bn is a first-class locale — the team has invested in this user without ever naming her in Idea.md. The liturgy is hers; the software's *frame* (dates, times, tone) isn't yet.
5. **The returning striver.** Keeps a streak, misses one sick day, sees zero, quits for a month. Zikr already fixed the plumbing (backdated entries rebuild streaks from history — CHANGELOG 1.3.0) but the psychology is still all-or-nothing.

## What the market says

- **Privacy is a purchase criterion in this category, not just a preference.** Users flee Muslim Pro over its data-selling scandal and ad/subscription bloat, and rally around Dhikr & Dua specifically for "totally free, no ads" ([r/islam thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/islam/comments/1f53au7/what_islamic_apps_do_you_recommend); the Bitsmedia location-data story was widely reported by Vice/Motherboard in 2020). "Everything stays on your device" is a *positioning weapon*.
- **Prayer times bundled with tasbih is a proven beloved pairing** — e.g. [Prayer Times & Tasbih](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=hasan.basics.prayertimestasbihapp) on Play; Deen Minder markets Athan+Adhkar as one daily routine. It is the single most common "one app instead of two" ask.
- **Offline astronomical prayer computation is trivially feasible at $0**: [adhan-js](https://github.com/batoulapps/adhan-js) (MIT, zero dependencies, browser-ready, includes the **Karachi method** — the standard used in Bangladesh — plus MWL/ISNA/Umm al-Qura and Hanafi/Shafi Asr).
- **Routines with streaks around adhkar is an established pattern**: Hisnul Muslim apps ship complete morning/evening collections ([App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com)); Niyat and similar counters pair adhkar with streak consistency. Users want the *consistency wrapper*, which Zikr partially has.
- **Khatma-style long-run completion tracking is its own proven app category** (Khatmah, Quranly, [Niyat's khatm-tracker comparison](https://niyatapp.com)) — completion of a large count over a long period is a cherished object. No direct evidence found for "1000x istighfar campaigns" as an app feature; that specific preset idea remains unproven.
- **Brittle streaks cause dropout; grace mechanics are the norm**: the Streaks app uses streak freeze/forgiveness, and "never miss twice" (Atomic Habits) is the standard framing ([Gaugius habit-tracker roundup](https://gaugius.com)). Even ad-laden counter apps advertise "less ads" and big count ceilings as selling points ([Play Store review snippet](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.trkstudio.tasbeehcounter&hl=en_US)) — "counts past 1000" is table stakes, which Zikr's 1.4.0 counting-past-target already matches.
- **Widgets/quick access is loved but structurally out of reach**: Muslim Pro ships home+lock-screen widgets ([their help center](https://support.muslimpro.com)); Idea.md explicitly accepts that PWAs have no home-screen widget. That kills the widget hypothesis as stated and reshapes it into in-app one-tap logging.
- **Bengali market signal is real but thin from search**: ihadis is a 5-star bn staple, printed prayer-time calendars persist as a cultural artifact (diaspora papers publish them), and bn-localized Muslim apps (Lunora) find audiences — search tooling was weak here, so this persona rests mainly on the repo's own bn investment.

## Opportunity set

1. **Post-salah mode** — *When I finish a prayer and open Zikr, I want the after-salah set ready to run so that I don't have to remember what to do or hunt for it.* Job: attach the app to a ritual that already has a perfect trigger (the salah itself). Local, offline prayer times via adhan-js (few KB gzipped, includes Karachi) detect "within N minutes after a prayer time" and surface the 33/33/34 flow; Android can add Web Push later (Idea.md's plan), iOS stays in-app ambient per its known limits. Fits differentiators: turns the existing counter + manual-entry into a *situated* flow. Evidence: catalog design; Idea.md's own deferred v1.1 "context suggestions by time"; competitor pairing praise above. Kano: **performer with delighter halo** (surprising-and-situated). **S** build (library + one home-state surface). Learn first: fake-door — show a mocked "It's Maghrib — start your after-salah set" state to 5 users.
2. **Routines as a first-class object (wird)** — *When I do my morning adhkar, I want one flow that carries me item-by-item with its own "done today," so the sequence itself is the goal, not five goal-rows.* The CHANGELOG 1.3.0 "Continue next zikr" button is this idea breaking through organically — today it's bolted onto per-zikr *plans*, which carry dates, deadlines and completion semantics that liturgical routines don't want (you don't "complete" morning adhkar by a date; you do it daily). A routine = ordered zikr refs + per-item counts (already in the catalog's `defaultTarget`) + a per-routine daily done + own streak. Reuses the counter wholesale. Kano: **performer**; delighter when time-anchored (morning/evening slots). **M**. Learn first: have 3 users run the catalog's morning cluster through the existing plan+continue flow and count where they stumble.
3. **Gentle restarts (streak forgiveness)** — *When I miss a day, I want to come back without the app telling me I failed, so a sick day doesn't end the practice.* Grace day / "resume today" framing / a one-tap "log yesterday?" prompt (backdated logging already works and already rebuilds streaks — CHANGELOG 1.3.0). This is mostly presentation on existing plumbing. Kano: **performer of the must-be-right kind** — its absence actively repels lapsed users, the app's most at-risk segment. **S**. Learn first: talk to 5 lapsed users (the group WhatsApp channels) about their last dropout moment.
4. **Curated pledges (campaigns over existing plans+groups)** — *When Ramadan starts or I take a 40-day istighfar pledge, I want a ready-made campaign I can start alone or as a family group, so I don't hand-build a plan.* Shapes the khatma insight into *presets/templates* over machinery that already exists (plans, groups, per-zikr targets, anonymous contributions) — e.g. "1000 Istighfar this Ramadan," "40 days of morning adhkar," a family pledge replacing the WhatsApp tally screenshot. Evidence: khatma category strength; tally-chart culture; groups already shipped. Demand for the *presets* specifically is unproven — cheap content, not new code. Kano: **delighter**. **S**. Learn first: post 2–3 pledge templates in a bn Muslim community group and see if anyone asks "where do I tap to join."
5. **Bengali-first ritual frame** — *When I open the app in bn, I want Hijri and Bangla dates, Dhaka prayer times, and copy that reads like home, so this feels made for me and not translated at me.* Hijri date on Home, Karachi as the default prayer method when locale is bn, bn tone pass on system copy. The catalog's 100% bn coverage shows intent; this completes it at the frame level. Kano: **delighter with moat properties** (competitors are English-first). **S/M**. Learn first: a 30-minute bn-language session with 2 diaspora users comparing the app against a printed সময়সূচী.
6. **Two-tap manual log at the ritual moment** — *When I've done 33 on my wooden misbaha, I want to log it before I stand up, so the physical practice still counts.* A Home quick-log row: zikr chip + count preset (33/100 per misbaha convention) + done. This is the PWA-legal reshape of the widget opportunity. Kano: **performer** for persona 1; strengthens the core differentiator rather than adding a new one. **S**. Learn first: card-sort with users on what belongs on Home's first screen.
7. **Rhythm pacing for long counts** — *When I'm on 100+ of something, I want a soft pulse to keep my place without looking, so long dhikr stays meditative.* Vibration cadence / breath-line animation. Evidence is thin (inference from "losing count" friction). Kano: speculative **delighter**. **M**. Defer.

## Top picks

1. **Post-salah mode (#1)** — the only opportunity where the catalog, the deferred Idea.md line ("context suggestions by time"), and competitor demand all point at the same gap; adhan-js makes it $0/offline/tiny; and it activates the app at the exact moment personas 1 and 2 already practice. Nothing else converts "local-first" from an engineering constraint into a felt product virtue.
2. **Routines as first-class object (#2)** — the "Continue next zikr" button proves users already try to run sequences through goal machinery; the catalog's 3x/7x cluster is routine-shaped data with no routine object. It's the structural fix that #1 then flows *into*.
3. **Gentle restarts (#3)** — smallest build, protects the retention mechanic the whole product leans on, and its evidence base (habit research + the existing backdated-streak plumbing) means it's mostly honest copy and one prompt.

These beat the rest because they compound (#1 supplies the moment, #2 the object, #3 the durability) while staying strictly inside the cost/offline/size constraints; campaigns (#4) and bn frame (#5) are strong follow-ons but ride on top of them.

## Cheapest next tests

- **Post-salah mode:** show 5 users (mix bn/en) a static mock of the Home state saying "It's Maghrib — your after-salah set" *without explaining it*; success = they recognize the moment unprompted. Then a one-week adhan-js spike (times only, no UI) to sanity-check calculation methods against a local mosque timetable.
- **Routines:** no new code — have 3 users run the catalog's morning set via an existing per-zikr plan with "Continue next zikr"; log every hesitation. If they ask "where's my morning adhkar today?" afterward, the object is real.
- **Gentle restarts:** message 5 users who lapsed (group channels are the natural recruiting pool) with one question: "what happened the day your streak broke?" Ship the grace framing behind a setting to 10 users and compare 7-day return by hand (no analytics — keep the privacy brand).
